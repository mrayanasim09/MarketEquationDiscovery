## Manuscript Information
- [x] **Abstract**: Forecasting CPI inflation accurately is crucial for central banks and economic agents. This study investigates the application of Graph Neural Networks (GNNs) on international trade networks to forecast consumer price inflation across 20 European economies. We demonstrate that modeling economies as interconnected nodes allows for the effective capture of cross-border inflationary spillovers. Using an expanding window evaluation framework, we compare our GNN approach against standard benchmark models. The results indicate significant improvements in forecast accuracy, confirmed via Diebold-Mariano tests.
- [x] **Keywords**: inflation forecasting, graph neural networks, trade networks, macroeconomic forecasting, Diebold-Mariano test, expanding window evaluation
- [x] **Highlights**:
  - Novel application of Graph Neural Networks to model global trade networks for macroeconomic forecasting.
  - Empirical demonstration of forecasting CPI inflation across 20 European economies with expanding window evaluation.
  - Significant predictive gains over traditional baseline models as verified by moving-block bootstrap Diebold-Mariano tests.
  - Comprehensive robustness checks utilizing probabilistic forecasting and multiple graph variants.
